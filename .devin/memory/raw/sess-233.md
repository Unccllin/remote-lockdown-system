## Outcomes
- What worked: - The proxy's request log is exactly the evidence needed: it shows the bytes arriving from outside the harness.

- What failed: - First probe failed on hostname mismatch (SAN is 10.0.2.2, I connected to 127.0.0.1) — correct behaviour, wrong test.
- What was unexpected: - The leaf lacked AKI entirely; EDK2 accepted it while OpenSSL 3 refused.
- The capture server had no guards on its own inputs.

## Learnings
- Verify generated certificates with a strict client, not with the one that happens to tolerate them.
- Put the chain check next to the pin in the generator's output.
- A TLS front is the smallest change when a consumer refuses plaintext.

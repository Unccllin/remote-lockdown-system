## What the run must show to close it

1. The serial log's pre-registered check, taken before anything else:
   `[OK] device_id from AK Name: …` — **not** `RC=0x9A2` (platform hierarchy taken away) and **not**
   `RC=0x18B` (define side), which are the two codes the owner-hierarchy change exists to eliminate.
2. `TPM2_Clear(RH_OWNER)` afterwards, then a second boot, must read as **unenrolled** rather than
   silently falling into the cached-verdict → denied-boot path (the D-131 ruling that makes the failure
   mode safe).

## 5. What the document gets wrong, and why it matters

1. **It under-sells the project's current buildability (C2, P3).** A reader would conclude the
   project cannot be verified or shipped from this machine and depends on one lab laptop. In fact
   every suite runs here and Windows builds in CI. The advice is still worth taking — the *document*
   `AGENTS.md` is stale (C1) and CI-based Windows builds were only proven this session — but the
   facts as stated are wrong.
2. **Its line numbers are stale.** S1 cites `worker.ts:1559`; the handler starts at 1638. S2 cites
   `:722`; the handler starts at 801. Somebody fixing from the document will edit the wrong place.
3. **S9 understates its own finding** (two response sites cited, four exist), and **S2 understates
   its cost** (it is N+1 reads per anonymous request, not one scan).

None of this weakens the security analysis, which is accurate and materially useful: S1 and S2 are
real, critical, and worth fixing before any client deployment.

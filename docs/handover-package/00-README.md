## How the transfer runs

| Phase | Duration | What happens | Gate to exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 — Decisions | 2 weeks | The three decisions in the covering letter are signed | Signed decision record |
| 1 — Escrow and accounts | 2 weeks | Keys sealed and deposited; accounts migrated or re-issued; signing identities moved | Custody schedule countersigned |
| 2 — Reproduction | 3 weeks | Your team builds and verifies the System on your own hardware, unaided | Clean build and full test sweep pass with our engineers observing only |
| 3 — Shadow operation | 8 weeks | Joint operation; your team leads, we advise | Two hardware windows and one restore drill completed by your team |
| 4 — Handover | 1 week | Named owners confirmed, acceptances signed, our access revoked | Signed acceptance form |

Total: 16 weeks from sign-off.

## Transmission and handling

- Documents 0–6 are delivered as PDF; document 3 (key custody) is delivered as PDF and in a
  machine-readable form so your key management system can ingest it. Document 0 carries no foreign
  currency and no foreign vendor reference: it is the sheet to circulate internally.
- **No credential value appears in any document in this package.** Document 3 records where each key
  lives and how it moves, never what it is. All values move in a separate, sealed channel agreed at
  Phase 1.
- Physical assets transfer in person or by insured courier with a signed asset receipt.
- Until Phase 4 completes, our access to your environments is read-only and time-boxed, and every access
  is recorded.
